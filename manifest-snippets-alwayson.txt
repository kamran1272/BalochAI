================================================================
BALOCH — ALWAYS-ON VOICE: AndroidManifest.xml snippets
================================================================
Paste the permission block INSIDE <manifest>, next to the other
<uses-permission> lines. Paste the <service> block INSIDE <application>,
next to the other <service> entries. Do NOT change anything else.

----------------------------------------------------------------
1) PERMISSIONS — paste inside <manifest> (with the other permissions)
----------------------------------------------------------------

    <!-- Always-on voice: foreground mic service (Android 14 rule) -->
    <uses-permission android:name="android.permission.FOREGROUND_SERVICE" />
    <uses-permission android:name="android.permission.FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MICROPHONE" />
    <!-- Keeps the CPU awake for the wake-word loop with the screen off -->
    <uses-permission android:name="android.permission.WAKE_LOCK" />
    <!-- Full-screen confirm notification for risky commands (screen on) -->
    <uses-permission android:name="android.permission.USE_FULL_SCREEN_INTENT" />
    <!-- Battery-optimisation exemption prompt (see ALWAYSON-DOCS-ADD.md) -->
    <uses-permission android:name="android.permission.REQUEST_IGNORE_BATTERY_OPTIMIZATIONS" />

----------------------------------------------------------------
2) SERVICE — paste inside <application> (with the other services)
----------------------------------------------------------------

        <!-- Always-on voice: offline "hey baloch" wake-word listener.
             Runs even with the screen off / phone locked. -->
        <service
            android:name=".service.BalochListenerService"
            android:exported="false"
            android:foregroundServiceType="microphone" />

----------------------------------------------------------------
NOTES
----------------------------------------------------------------
- android:foregroundServiceType="microphone" is REQUIRED on targetSdk 34:
  starting a mic foreground service without it throws
  SecurityException (ForegroundServiceStartNotAllowedException family).
- android:exported="false": only the Baloch app itself may start/stop it.
- RECORD_AUDIO and POST_NOTIFICATIONS are already declared elsewhere in
  the manifest; both are runtime permissions the user must grant.
- No <intent-filter> on purpose — the service is started only via
  explicit intents (BalochListenerService.start/stop()).
