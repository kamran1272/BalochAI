POWER-FEATURES MANIFEST SNIPPETS — merge into app/src/main/AndroidManifest.xml
================================================================================
DO NOT create a second manifest. Insert each block at the marked point.
RECORD_AUDIO and POST_NOTIFICATIONS already exist — do NOT re-add them.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
BLOCK 1 — permissions. INSERTION POINT: right after the existing
    <uses-permission android:name="android.permission.RECORD_AUDIO" />
line (in the permissions group at the top).

    <!-- Driving mode: see the SMS to auto-reply (body parsed from the
         broadcast PDUs — READ_SMS is NOT needed) -->
    <uses-permission android:name="android.permission.RECEIVE_SMS" />
    <!-- Driving mode: send the "I'm driving" auto-reply -->
    <uses-permission android:name="android.permission.SEND_SMS" />
    <!-- Driving mode (optional, declared for future auto-detect):
         ACTIVITY_RECOGNITION is NOT used by the current build — driving
         mode is a manual toggle + auto-off timer by design. Declared so a
         future auto-detect can light up without a manifest change. -->
    <uses-permission android:name="android.permission.ACTIVITY_RECOGNITION" />

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
BLOCK 2 — SMS receiver. INSERTION POINT: inside <application>, right after
the existing receiver blocks:

    <!-- Driving mode: auto-reply + read-aloud on incoming SMS.
         exported=true is REQUIRED on Android 12+ for SMS_RECEIVED.
         The receiver no-ops unless driving mode is ON. -->
    <receiver
        android:name=".power.DrivingSmsReceiver"
        android:exported="true"
        android:permission="android.permission.BROADCAST_SMS">
        <intent-filter android:priority="999">
            <action android:name="android.provider.Telephony.SMS_RECEIVED" />
        </intent-filter>
    </receiver>

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
BLOCK 3 — charge receiver. INSERTION POINT: inside <application>, after
the DrivingSmsReceiver block:

    <!-- Charge-complete alert: plug-in / unplug announcements. -->
    <receiver
        android:name=".power.ChargeAlertReceiver"
        android:exported="false">
        <intent-filter>
            <action android:name="android.intent.action.ACTION_POWER_CONNECTED" />
            <action android:name="android.intent.action.ACTION_POWER_DISCONNECTED" />
        </intent-filter>
    </receiver>

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
BLOCK 4 — notification-listener service. INSERTION POINT: inside
<application>, after the receiver blocks:

    <!-- Driving mode: reads other apps' notifications aloud while driving.
         Needs BIND_NOTIFICATION_LISTENER and the user granting
         "Notification access" once in Android Settings; without it the
         service never starts and only SMS are read aloud. -->
    <service
        android:name=".power.DrivingNotifications"
        android:exported="false"
        android:permission="android.permission.BIND_NOTIFICATION_LISTENER">
        <intent-filter>
            <action android:name="android.service.notification.NotificationListenerService" />
        </intent-filter>
    </service>

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
