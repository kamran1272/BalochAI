SCHEDULED-TASK MANIFEST SNIPPETS — merge into app/src/main/AndroidManifest.xml
================================================================================
DO NOT create a second manifest. Insert each block at the marked point.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
BLOCK 1 — permission. INSERTION POINT: right after the existing
    <uses-permission android:name="android.permission.SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM" />
line (in the "<!-- Exact alarms -->" group).

    <!-- Re-arm scheduled tasks after reboot -->
    <uses-permission android:name="android.permission.RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED" />

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
BLOCK 2 — receivers. INSERTION POINT: inside <application>, right after the
existing ReminderReceiver block:

        <!-- Fires scheduled reminders -->
        <receiver
            android:name=".engine.ReminderReceiver"
            android:exported="false" />

    <!-- One-shot scheduled tasks (exact AlarmManager alarms) -->
    <receiver
        android:name=".schedule.TaskFireReceiver"
        android:exported="false" />

    <!-- Re-arms all enabled scheduled tasks after a reboot -->
    <receiver
        android:name=".schedule.RescheduleReceiver"
        android:exported="true">
        <intent-filter>
            <action android:name="android.intent.action.BOOT_COMPLETED" />
        </intent-filter>
    </receiver>

Notes:
- TaskFireReceiver must stay exported="false": only Baloch's own alarms fire it.
- RescheduleReceiver must be exported="true": the SYSTEM sends BOOT_COMPLETED.
- No service declaration needed: TaskWorker runs inside WorkManager's own
  infrastructure (work-runtime-ktx 2.9.0 is already a dependency).
- SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM is already declared; no new alarm permission needed.
